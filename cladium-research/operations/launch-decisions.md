# Launch decisions the business owner must make

This is the short decision list that unlocks a real ordering and booking launch.

## Orders

1. **Confirmed:** takeaway only. Home delivery is not currently offered; leave all delivery configuration disabled.
2. Who receives new takeaway requests, what statuses do staff use, and how quickly should they accept/reject and provide a collection estimate?
3. Which payment methods may staff arrange at collection? Online payment stays disabled until separately approved and integrated.
4. Are the listed prices inclusive or exclusive of government tax and service charge? Specify each rate, effective date, and calculation; otherwise the system must say charges are unconfigured.
5. If delivery is introduced in a future project: approve zones, fees, minimum order, fulfilment ownership, timing, privacy, testing, and a separate launch flag. It is not a launch requirement.

## Table booking

1. **Confirmed regular timing:** 12 pm–12 am. Confirm whether this changes by weekday, public holiday, or season.
2. Tables/areas, guest capacities, slot durations, and seating preferences.
3. How far ahead can guests book? Is a deposit required? General seating is ample; treehouse capacity is limited.
4. Rules for cancellations, late arrivals, no-shows, and large groups.
5. Who confirms bookings and through which channel?

## Birthday and event policy

- **Confirmed:** décor is available from PKR 8,000; it must be quoted and confirmed by staff.
- **Confirmed:** the café does not provide cakes and outside food is not allowed.
- Decide decor packages, allowable exceptions, lead time, deposit rules, and the staff member who approves each request.

## Chatbot and WhatsApp

1. The official WhatsApp Business Account and the staff escalation phone/team.
2. English and Urdu are mandatory. Approve the Urdu translations, Roman Urdu behavior, pronunciation guide, and separately evaluated English/Urdu voice profiles.
3. The approved answer to dietary/allergy questions—otherwise the bot must escalate.
4. Any prohibited responses or sensitive complaint/refund process.
5. Whether browser voice recording will remain disabled (recommended). Enabling it later requires separate consent, retention, and deletion approval.
6. WhatsApp Cloud API is future/flagged; approve WABA ownership, templates, opt-in/opt-out, webhook security, and staff operations before enabling it.

## Marketing and governance

1. Meta Business Portfolio owner and admin access.
2. Which Pixel/Dataset and ad account will own conversion events?
3. Consent/banner wording and privacy-policy legal review.
4. Which staff member owns menu updates, gallery approvals, and campaign publication?
5. Which owner/manager/order/booking/auditor accounts are required, and who administers MFA and access removal?
6. Approve retention/deletion periods, incident owner, backup/restore objectives, and launch rollback decision-maker.
