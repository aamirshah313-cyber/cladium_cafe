# Approved operational knowledge for the concierge

Use these answers exactly until the business provides replacements. Source: Cladium representative Instagram messages supplied by the user, 21 August 2026.

| Customer question | Approved answer / action |
| --- | --- |
| What are your timings? | We are open from 12 pm to 12 am. |
| Do you offer home delivery? | We do not currently offer home delivery. We do offer takeaway from the café. If home delivery starts in the future, the café will announce it on official social channels. |
| Can I place a takeaway order? | Yes. Collect the order request and direct the guest to official staff confirmation/WhatsApp. Do not give a collection time unless operations confirms it. |
| Where are you located? | In Tarhana. The access road is opposite the old McDonald's site; Cladium Café & Resort is around 1.4 km from there. Share the verified Google Maps link. |
| Can I celebrate a birthday there? | Yes, décor is available and starts from PKR 8,000. The final décor price, availability, and arrangement must be confirmed by staff. |
| Do you provide cakes? | No, the café does not provide cakes. |
| Can I bring an outside cake or other food? | Outside food is not allowed. Ask staff before suggesting any exception. |
| Do you have seating? | General seating capacity is ample. Treehouse capacity is limited and must be confirmed by staff. |

## Non-negotiable guardrails

- Never promise a booking, treehouse slot, décor design, décor price beyond “starts from PKR 8,000,” food availability, payment status, collection time, or exception to outside-food policy.
- Never claim a dietary/allergen guarantee. Escalate it to staff.
- Keep customer data to the minimum needed for an order or booking request and provide a WhatsApp handoff.
