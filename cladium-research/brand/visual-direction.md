# Preliminary visual direction

This is a design direction, not a replacement for the café's source artwork or formal brand guidelines.

## Brand impression

Luxury mountain hospitality: dark, tranquil, botanical, and warm—more private retreat than conventional fast-food café. The site should use spacious editorial layouts, quietly moving landscape/ambience imagery, restrained animation, and a gold-accented conversion path.

## Evidence from the supplied official mark

The supplied mark combines a midnight-green field, a **soft antique-gold** mountain/lakeside-cabin crest, a refined high-contrast serif `CLADIŪM` wordmark, and the descriptor `CAFÉ & RESORT`. The retained tagline is **Relax • Refresh • Reconnect**.

## Type direction

- Display: a high-contrast editorial serif for luxury and place-making.
- Interface/body: a calm contemporary sans serif with excellent Urdu support if bilingual content is needed.
- Keep the official logo as supplied artwork; never recreate it with a substitute font.

## Image art direction

Prioritize wide mountain vistas, dusk-to-night warmth, timber/stone textures, atmospheric garden seating, plated dishes, and authentic guest moments. The supplied garden photograph establishes a real signature setting: mature trees, lawn paths, intimate warm lights, and a relaxed outdoor counter/seating area. Avoid generic stock café photographs. Photograph food on a controlled neutral/forest-toned set so menu cards feel consistent.

## Lighter menu-carousel direction

The uploaded menu reference demonstrates a clear product-selection interaction, but Cladium's implementation should be visibly lighter and quieter than its dark source screen. Use the official dark forest only for the logo, navigation text, primary buttons, and small structural accents; use warm ivory and mist surfaces for the dominant menu canvas.
