# Materials needed to complete the design and build pack

Give these directly from the business owner or an authorized administrator. They are more accurate and safer than trying to scrape social platforms.

## Brand and media

- Original logo files: SVG, AI, PDF, or transparent PNG; all approved variants.
- 20–40 approved high-resolution photos: exterior, scenery, interior zones, food, events, and staff/service.
- Original menu PDF/print artwork and all food photography with permission to reuse.
- Brand rules: approved colors, fonts, tone of voice, and Urdu/English spelling preferences.

## Commerce and booking

- Current menu spreadsheet with item name, category, description, price (PKR), photo, availability, variants/add-ons, allergens, and tax/service-charge rules.
- Takeaway acceptance/decline, preparation, collection estimate, cancellation, staff ownership, payment-at-collection options, and operational hours by day.
- Booking policy: zones/tables, guest limits, available time slots, booking lead time, deposits, cancellation/no-show rules, and special-event packages.

Future-only: collect delivery areas, fees, minimum order, fulfilment/timing, and related policies only if the owner starts a separately approved delivery project. Delivery must remain disabled for launch.

## Places and contact

- Exact Google Maps share link / Place ID, street address, phone number, official email, and any existing web domain.
- Official WhatsApp Business number and desired escalation team/hours.
- Primary public phone-number decision, official email/domain, and named incident/launch contacts.

## Meta access (owner only)

- Admin/partner access to the Meta Business Portfolio that owns the Facebook Page and Instagram account.
- The linked WhatsApp Business Account, WABA phone number, and approved display name.
- Ad account, Meta Pixel/Dataset, catalog (if used), and payment/billing owner.

## Recommended owner exports

- Meta Business Suite: Page and Instagram insights export (last 12 months).
- A ZIP of all original post/reel assets and captions chosen for the website; do not rely on screen captures.
- Restaurant's current POS/delivery export for the authoritative menu and price list.
- Owner-reviewed Urdu copy/pronunciation list and consented English/Urdu voice-test participants.
