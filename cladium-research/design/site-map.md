# Site map and content hierarchy

```text
Home
├── Our story / mountain garden atmosphere
├── Signature experiences
├── Featured menu items
├── Gallery teaser
├── Visit teaser + map
├── Order dining → Menu → Cart → Order request → Staff confirmation
└── Request a table → Request review → Staff confirmation

Menu
├── Starters and soups
├── Sandwiches and burgers
├── Steaks and exclusive beef entrées
├── Desi cuisine and BBQ
├── Italian and Chinese
├── Beverages and sides
└── Cart

Visit
├── Directions / map
├── Hours
├── Contact / official WhatsApp
└── Garden, family and group-visit details

Concierge
├── Menu recommendation
├── Ordering help
├── Booking help
├── Visit and directions
└── WhatsApp staff handoff
```

## Home-page narrative

1. **Arrival:** an atmospheric outdoor-garden hero, the real Cladium crest, and two clear conversion actions.
2. **Pause:** a short promise built around `Relax • Refresh • Reconnect`.
3. **Taste:** a selective, premium menu story—signature steaks, desi classics, Turkish BBQ, and crafted drinks—without trying to display every item at once.
4. **Place:** the garden setting, authentic venue photography, access information, and map.
5. **Action:** Order, book, or continue the conversation on official WhatsApp.
